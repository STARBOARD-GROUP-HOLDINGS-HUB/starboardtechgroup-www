Code of Entity Separation

Starboard Group Holdings operates with absolute fidelity to the law and corporate governance. To ensure there is no "active concert" or "commingling" of interests, SGH enforces a rigorous Code of Entity Separation.

01

Separate Entity Banking & Financial Ledgers

Each entity under SGH maintains its own distinct federal Employer Identification Number (EIN), its own separate commercial bank accounts, and dedicated QuickBooks accounting profiles. Under no circumstances are corporate funds co-mingled, nor does STAR, Salty Marine, or UpgradeGyro assume or guarantee debts of Starboard Yacht Group LLC.

02

Independent Operational Missions

Operations, labor, and services are strictly non-overlapping. Starboard Yacht Group LLC performs heavy mechanical installations and refits; STAR performs R&D and software development; Salty Marine engineered advanced battery solutions; and UpgradeGyro handles diagnostic evaluations. No Seakeeper installations or dealer activity ever occur under STAR, Salty, UpgradeGyro, or HelmLegal.

03

Independent Branding & Distinct Disclaimers

Each sub-brand maintains its own isolated digital footprint, marketing channels, and customer funnels. To eliminate any possibility of consumer confusion, independent diagnostic or research-focused pages feature prominent, conspicuous disclaimers explaining their unaffiliated status with third-party manufacturers, fully complying with standard Lanham Act safe harbors and nominative fair use doctrine.

"Common ownership or shared service operations do not establish an agency or active concert relationship under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 65(d)(2). Injunctions are restricted solely to the named parties and those proven to be their legal alter-egos." — Factual Black-Letter Law (Alemite Trilogy)
